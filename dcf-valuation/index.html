Rows vary WACC; columns vary the terminal assumption. The outlined cell is your base case.

Unlevered free cash flow = EBIT × (1 − tax) + D&A − capex − increase in net working capital. Terminal value uses the Gordon growth formula on year-6 cash flow, or an EV/EBITDA multiple on year-5 EBITDA.

With the mid-year convention, forecast cash flows are discounted at t − 0.5. A perpetuity-growth terminal value is discounted at 4.5 years, an exit-multiple terminal value at 5 years. Sample figures are illustrative and describe no real company.
